# Model Just Did a Faceplant 💀

**Topic:** Model failed in production  
**Platform:** Instagram  
**Tone:** Meme style

## Content Idea
The production deployment of our new mobile app? Let's just say the 'send' button consistently failed, and my user testing group only sent angry emojis. 😡

## Hook
Send button failed. Repeatedly. 🤦‍♂️

## Reel Script
Okay, here’s a 30-second Instagram Reel script in a meme-style, aiming for humor and a quick impact.

---

**Reel Title Suggestion:** “Model Meltdown”

**(Total Time: Approx. 30 seconds)**

**Scene 1 (Hook) - 0-7 seconds**
*   **Visual:** Close-up shot of someone frantically pressing a large, red "Send" button on a computer screen.  Zoom in on frustrated face. Overlay text: “Send button failed. Repeatedly. 🤦‍♂️”
*   **Voiceover:** (Fast, exasperated tone) “You know when you press ‘Send’ and… nothing?”

**Scene 2 (Explain) - 7-15 seconds**
*   **Visual:** Quick cuts showing: a stressed-looking project manager on a video call, a designer frantically refreshing a webpage, a confused client messaging. Overlay text: “Production team levels of chaos.”
*   **Voiceover:** “It’s like the internet just… decided to take a nap.  The model?  Not so much.”

**Scene 3 (Result) - 15-23 seconds**
*   **Visual:** A still image of a very awkward, slightly blurry, and clearly low-quality image of a product - maybe a shoe or a phone - with a confused expression superimposed on it.  Add subtle glitch effects. Overlay text: “Client Feedback: ‘Interesting choice.’”
*   **Voiceover:** (Dryly) “Turns out, a ‘high-res’ model is actually… high-res.”

**Scene 4 (Closing) - 23-30 seconds**
*   **Visual:** Close-up of the blurry product image again, with a single, giant, exaggerated eye blinking slowly. Overlay text: "Send help."
*   **Voiceover:** (Calm, deadpan) “We’re accepting applications.”
