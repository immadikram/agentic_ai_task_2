## Caption
The initial results were fantastic – 88% precision on my NLP sentiment analysis project, built with a robust training dataset and rigorous validation. Then, we deployed to a live test group of 500 users. Suddenly, precision plummeted to 62%. 🤯 My supervisor, understandably, experienced a brief but intense period of… let’s call it “assessment.” 

It highlighted the critical difference between controlled lab environments and the messy, unpredictable reality of real-time data. We spent the following week painstakingly investigating data drift, user behaviour variations, and subtle biases we hadn't accounted for.  It was a brutal, but invaluable, experience in understanding the entire lifecycle of a deployed model. 

What are some of the biggest deployment surprises *you’ve* encountered?
